03 · Ventures & Ecosystem

Three ventures built around automotive culture.

Founded 2021 · Lagos & UK Partners Founder & CEO

Zeuswrap

Following his very first car wrap on a Mikano Geely Coolray in 2020, Leslie built Nigeria's premier 24/7 vehicle customization studio. With 600+ vehicles wrapped in Nigeria and ongoing work with partners in the UK, Zeuswrap delivers colour-change wraps, self-healing PPF, ceramic tinting, tyre lettering, and fleet branding.

First Wrap: Mikano Geely Coolray 600+ Vehicles Wrapped Working With Partners in the UK
Motorsport Interior Art Founder & Creative Lead

Zeus3D

Born from Leslie's passion for Formula 1 and experience driving open-wheel race cars on the European Grand Prix circuit, Zeus3D creates full-scale motorsport wall sculptures and bespoke automotive art installations for luxury residences, penthouses, and executive offices.

F1 Wall Sculptures Architectural Auto Art Bespoke Liveries
Mobility Tech · Live in Lagos Founder & Product Lead

Handwrk

An on-demand roadside-assistance web application (usehandwrk.com) engineered for Lagos drivers. Handwrk matches stranded motorists with verified vulcanizers and tow trucks within minutes, featuring live GPS tracking, transparent itemised quotes, and Paystack escrow protection.
